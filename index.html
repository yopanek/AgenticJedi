OpenClaw (openclaw.ai) is a powerful open-source AI personal assistant that runs on your own hardware. It connects to any chat app (WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, etc.) and can manage your email, calendar, browse the web, run code, and more — all using AI models like Claude or GPT-4. It's free software, but complex to set up. That's where we come in.

AI model API costs are separate from our management fee and are billed directly to you by the provider (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, etc.). Typical costs range from $10–$50/month depending on usage. We help you configure cost-efficient settings, and you can also use free local models via Ollama to minimize costs.

We use secure SSH and encrypted tunnels to access your machine for setup and maintenance. We never store your credentials or API keys on our servers. After setup, you can revoke our access if you prefer — though keeping it enabled lets us provide ongoing support and monitoring.
