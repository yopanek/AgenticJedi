FAQ

Common questions

We support any Linux-based VPS. We recommend DigitalOcean, Hetzner, or Vultr for their reliability and pricing — typically $6–$20/mo for a suitable instance. You own and pay for the VPS directly; we handle all the setup and management on top of it.

OpenClaw (openclaw.ai) is a powerful open-source AI personal assistant that runs on your own server. It connects to any chat app (WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, etc.) and can manage your email, calendar, browse the web, run code, and more — all using AI models like Claude or GPT-4. It's free software, but complex to set up. That's where we come in.

Tailscale creates an encrypted peer-to-peer mesh VPN between your devices and your VPS. With Tailscale configured, your OpenClaw instance is completely invisible to the public internet — only devices on your private Tailscale network can reach it. This dramatically reduces your attack surface.

Caddy is a modern reverse proxy that automatically provisions and renews TLS certificates (HTTPS) via Let's Encrypt. We configure it to sit in front of your OpenClaw instance, handling secure subdomain routing, rate limiting, and security headers — so your agent is accessible over a clean, secure HTTPS URL without any manual certificate management.

AI model API costs are separate from our management fee and are billed directly to you by the provider (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, etc.). Typical costs range from $10–$50/month depending on usage. We help you configure cost-efficient settings, and you can also use free local models via Ollama to minimize costs.

We use secure SSH with key-based authentication to access your VPS for setup and maintenance. We never store your credentials or API keys on our servers. After setup, you can revoke our SSH access if you prefer — though keeping it enabled lets us provide ongoing monitoring and support.

Yes. Cancel anytime with no penalties. Your OpenClaw instance will remain running on your VPS — you just won't receive managed updates or support going forward. We'll provide documentation so you can maintain it yourself if needed.
